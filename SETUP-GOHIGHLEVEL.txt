HOW TO CONNECT THE NEW QUIZ TO GOHIGHLEVEL
==========================================

When someone finishes the quiz on the new website, the site sends their
answers to GoHighLevel. GoHighLevel needs a workflow that receives them and
creates the contact. Setting it up takes about 10 minutes in your account.


1. MAKE THE WORKFLOW
   - In GoHighLevel go to Automation > Workflows.
   - Click "+ Create Workflow" and choose "Start from Scratch".
   - Click "Add New Trigger", search for "Inbound Webhook", and pick it.
   - It shows a webhook URL. Copy it.

   Note: Inbound Webhook is a "premium" trigger. Depending on your plan,
   GoHighLevel may charge a small amount each time it runs.


2. PUT THE URL INTO THE WEBSITE
   - Open  prototype\index.html  in a text editor (Notepad works).
   - Search for:   data-webhook-url=""
   - Paste the URL between the quotes, like this:
       data-webhook-url="https://services.leadconnectorhq.com/hooks/....."
   - Save the file.


3. SEND ONE TEST SIGN-UP
   - Open the website, click "YES! I WANT TO JOIN FOR FREE", and finish the
     quiz with your own email.
   - Back in the workflow, open the trigger, click "Fetch Sample Requests",
     pick the request that just arrived, and save the trigger.


4. CREATE THE CONTACT
   - Add the action "Create Contact" (or "Create/Update Contact").
   - Using the sample request, map:
       First Name  ->  first_name
       Email       ->  email
   - Optional: to see the quiz answers on the contact, create custom fields
     (Settings > Custom Fields) and map goal, commitment, hardgainer, ready.
   - Optional: add a tag such as "30-day-challenge".


5. KEEP DOING WHAT THE OLD QUIZ DID
   - Open the workflow the old quiz starts today (its trigger is probably
     "Survey Submitted").
   - Add the same steps (for example the email with the free invitation) to
     the new workflow, after "Create Contact".


6. TURN IT ON
   - Switch the workflow from Draft to Publish and save.
   - Do one more test sign-up and check that the contact shows up and the
     invitation email arrives.


WHAT THE WEBSITE SENDS FOR EACH SIGN-UP
   first_name   e.g. "Sam"
   email        e.g. "sam@example.com"
   goal         "Get shredded", "Put on muscle mass" or "Both"
   commitment   "Yes, absolutely" or "Not sure"
   hardgainer   "Yes"
   ready        "Yes"
   source       "30-Day Challenge quiz"
   page         the web address of the page

People who answer "No" to the hardgainer question or the "ready to get
started" question see a goodbye message and are never sent, because the quiz
stops before it asks for their email.


GOOD TO KNOW
   - Until the URL is pasted in, the quiz still works but sends nothing.
   - If GoHighLevel can't be reached, the person sees "Something went wrong.
     Please try again." and their answers stay filled in.
   - The webhook URL can be seen by anyone who looks at the page's code. The
     quiz has a hidden spam trap that stops simple bots. If junk sign-ups
     still show up, that's the time to add stronger spam protection.
